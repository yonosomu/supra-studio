ADAPTATION / CHANGES
Geometry normalized to metre-like studio coordinates; main body subdivided
with creases and preserved boundaries. Hood, targa panel and doors partitioned
into selectable groups. Roof headliner patch removed to make targa opening
visible. Imported wheels attached to existing mechanical wheel groups.
Source brake/exhaust duplicates and front plate removed. Missing original
texture references replaced by self-contained procedural materials and paint;
front signal colors adjusted. Headlamp internals and front intake slats added.
Earlier custom 2JZ engine, chassis and educational drivetrain animation integrated.
Lighting, reflection, Russian controls, cutaway, selection, removal, exploded view
and a fully embedded offline HTML application added.
This is an adaptation, not the author's unmodified original model.
The visual base remains A80 1993; a factory-accurate 1998 conversion is not claimed.

white five-spoke, polished chrome deep-dish, bronze ten-spoke.
Original source tires retained; original stock rim geometry remains selectable.

Road animation and wheel-speed-linked presentation retained.
